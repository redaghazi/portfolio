Food & wellness

Ramadan Campaign – Yumi Matcha

Ramadan content planArabic copywritingHealth & wellness

Project overview

A Ramadan content plan for Yumi Matcha, with a cohesive visual identity that reflects the spirit of the season. Content is written in Arabic.

Strategy

Position matcha as a beneficial, relevant choice during fasting by highlighting its health benefits and showing how it fits into daily Ramadan routines.

The objective

Shift audience perception and encourage consistency, showing that matcha remains a valuable part of the lifestyle during Ramadan and not only on regular days.

Creative concept

A seasonal identity for the whole feed, built around the idea “Ramadan, with your daily matcha”, with a recurring Ramadan visual language across every post.

Content strategy & copywriting

The content mixed educational, emotional, and visually engaging elements to strengthen the brand connection and drive continuous engagement. Headlines are written as questions in conversational Arabic, so the post starts the conversation the audience is already having.

وش أفضل وقت لشرب الماتشا في رمضان؟
Educational hook: answers a real question about timing
كيف تساعدنا الماتشا في حرق الدهون؟
Health-benefit angle for fasting routines
رمضانك أحلى مع يومي ماتشا
Emotional, routine-based message
ليش طريقة تحضير الماتشا تفرق في الطعم والفائدة؟
Educational hook: preparation method

Creative execution

A nine-post grid of designs combining product shots, Ramadan lanterns and crescent moon motifs, and lifestyle imagery, all in one consistent visual system.

My role

I developed the Ramadan content plan, including the strategy, the content direction, and the visual identity of the campaign.

Sports apparel

Sports Wave

World Cup pre-launchFootball fansApparelArabic copywriting

Project overview

A strategic pre-launch campaign for Sports Wave, a football apparel brand, planned ahead of the World Cup season for fans who support their national teams.

Strategy

Position the brand as the go-to destination for fans who want to express their loyalty through high-quality apparel that combines comfort, style, and authenticity.

Creative concept

A strong, fan-driven visual identity that captures the excitement and emotional connection supporters feel with their national teams, with official-inspired kits and fan jerseys as the core product.

Tone of voice

Bold, celebratory, and engaging, designed to resonate with football enthusiasts and enhance their match-day experience.

Content strategy & copywriting

Short, energetic Arabic lines work like chants. They speak to the fan’s anticipation and put the jersey at the center of the match-day feeling.

جاهزين لكأس العالم؟
Launch hook: builds anticipation
جاهز للمباراة
Match-day readiness
حماس إسبانيا يبدأ هنا
Team-specific fan message
طقم البرازيل وصل
Product-arrival announcement

Creative execution

A dynamic, energetic visual direction: a stadium hero visual with the trophy, plus individual kit and jersey posts for each national team, using a gold-and-black palette for the immersive atmosphere of global football events.

My role

I developed this campaign as a strategic pre-launch plan, covering the concept, the visual direction, and the brand positioning.
